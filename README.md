# Rank Ledger

A record of my work on high-rank elliptic curves: the constructions, the calculations, and the evidence behind each claim.

Most of this work begins in [Rank Hunter](https://github.com/crhillresearch/rank-hunter). Each entry here is meant to stand on its own, with a readable account of the result and enough exact data for someone else to check it.

## Entries

| Entry | Result | Materials |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [From 17 to 19](rank-19-genus-one-base-change/) | Two quadratic covers add two sections over a genus-one base, giving infinitely many rational curves of rank at least 19. | Proof, exact data, verifier; paper: [HTML](https://hillaxiom.com/papers/k3-19-sections.html) · [PDF](https://hillaxiom.com/pdf/k3-19-sections.pdf) |
| [C2 family of rank at least 11](c2-rank-11-genus-one-family/) | Two quadratic covers add sections over a genus-one base, giving rank at least 11 with C2 torsion. | Proof, exact data, certificate, SageMath verifier |
| [C4 family of rank at least 6](c4-rank-6-genus-one-family/) | Two quadratic covers of Elkies's C4 K3 surface add sections over a positive-rank genus-one base. | Proof, exact data, certificate, SageMath verifier |
| [C4 q1+q3 family of rank at least 6](c4-rank-6-genus-one-q1-q3-family/) | A different pair of quadratic covers of the same Elkies surface gives another positive-rank genus-one base. | Proof, exact data, six worked specializations, certificate, SageMath verifier |
